Delete Your Healix Account

Healix allows you to permanently delete your account and associated cloud data at any time.

Delete your account from the app

  1. Open the Healix app.
  2. Sign in to your account.
  3. Go to Settings.
  4. Select Account.
  5. Tap Delete Account.
  6. Confirm the deletion request.

If you cannot access the app

Email our support team with the email address associated with your Healix account.

Email: your@email.com

Data deleted

  • Your Healix account
  • User profile
  • Cloud synchronized health data
  • Nutrition logs
  • Workout history
  • AI Coach history
  • Application preferences linked to your account

Data retained

Some information may be temporarily retained for legal compliance, fraud prevention, security, backup recovery, or as required by applicable law. Backups are automatically removed within 30 days.

Processing Time

Account deletion requests are normally processed immediately. Complete removal from backups may take up to 30 days.

Health Connect

Deleting your Healix account does not delete data stored in Android Health Connect. You can manage or remove Health Connect permissions and data directly through Android Health Connect settings.
