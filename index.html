Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

Healix ("the App") is developed and maintained by SJTECH. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and your rights.

Information We Collect

The App may collect the following information only after your permission where required.

Data Type Purpose
Health Connect Data Display health metrics, generate wellness insights, trends, and personalized recommendations.
Profile Information Age, height, weight, gender, and health goals used for calculations.
Nutrition Logs Meals, calories, water intake, and nutrition history.
Voice Input Voice commands used only for converting speech into meal or hydration logs.
AI Queries Questions you ask the AI Health Coach.
Device Information Basic technical information for troubleshooting and app performance.
Location Information Used to provide location-aware health features, activity tracking, weather-aware recommendations (if applicable), and improve user experience. Location is collected only with your permission.
Notification Information The App may request notification access to deliver health reminders, wellness alerts, progress updates, and to support optional notification-based features enabled by the user.

Health Data Access

If you connect Health Connect, the App may access health information such as:

  • Steps
  • Distance
  • Calories Burned
  • Sleep
  • Heart Rate
  • Heart Rate Variability (HRV)
  • Exercise Sessions
  • Recovery Metrics

Health data is accessed only after you grant permission through Android Health Connect. You may revoke permissions at any time from Health Connect settings.

How We Use Your Data

Your information is used only to:

  • Provide personalized health insights
  • Generate AI wellness recommendations
  • Calculate health metrics
  • Track progress over time
  • Improve app reliability and performance
  • Enable voice-based food and hydration logging

Artificial Intelligence

The App may use Google's Gemini AI service to generate personalized health insights and convert natural language into structured health logs.

Only the information necessary to provide the requested AI feature is sent securely to Google's AI service.

AI-generated responses are informational only and should not be considered medical advice.

Data Storage

Most health information is stored locally on your device using Android's local database. If cloud AI features are used, only the minimum information required for processing is transmitted securely.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share limited information only with trusted service providers required to operate app features, including:

  • Google Gemini AI (AI-generated insights)
  • Google Health Connect (health data access)

We do not share your health information with advertisers.

Permissions

The App may request permissions including:

  • Health Connect
  • Location (Approximate and/or Precise)
  • Microphone (voice logging)
  • Internet (AI processing)
  • Notifications (optional reminders)

Permissions are requested only when required for a specific feature and may be revoked at any time through your device settings.

Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures including encrypted network communication (HTTPS/TLS) to protect information transmitted between your device and our services.

Data Retention

Your locally stored health information remains on your device until you delete it or uninstall the application.

You may delete your data at any time from within the App (if supported) or by clearing the application's storage or uninstalling the App.

Your Rights

You may:

  • Access your stored information
  • Delete your local data
  • Revoke Health Connect permissions
  • Disable AI features
  • Disable microphone access

Location Access

The App may request access to your device's location while the application is in use. Location access is requested only with your permission and is used solely for features that require location information, such as activity tracking, walking or running routes, or location-based wellness recommendations (where available).

You can revoke location permission at any time from your device's Settings.

Notification Access

The App may request permission to send notifications and, where applicable, access notification-related features provided by Android to support health reminders, goal progress, medication reminders, hydration reminders, workout notifications, and other wellness features.

Notification permissions are optional and can be disabled at any time through your device settings.

Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Medical Disclaimer

Healix is intended for general health, fitness, and wellness purposes only. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, or emergency services. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before making medical decisions.

Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes become effective immediately after they are published on this page.
